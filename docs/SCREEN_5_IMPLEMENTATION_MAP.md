# Screen 5 implementation map — Decide and defend award

This is a derived implementation map. The active decision ledger is the authority; this document adds no new product requirements.

## 1. Screen objective

Enable the procurement manager to review Aera's award recommendation proposal against the approved evaluation basis, understand the explicit trade-offs and evidence, approve or correct the rationale, and explicitly approve the award.

The screen must end with `award-approved` and a durable award record in the prototype. Aera proposes; the procurement manager decides.

## 2. Entry and exit

**Entry:** Screen 4 has a completed comparison, the buyer has acknowledged that the comparison and resulting analysis are accurate and sufficient for award recommendation, and the comparison record is available.

**Exit:** The buyer explicitly approves a valid supplier and non-empty rationale. The system records the approved RFx/version, comparison status, supplier, criteria basis, rationale, approver action, timestamp/record ID, and evidence references.

## 3. User flow within the screen

1. See that the comparison is the basis for this decision.
2. Ask Aera for the recommendation proposal.
3. Review the proposed supplier and the approved decision criteria.
4. See the trade-offs that support the proposal.
5. Open the evidence behind the proposal and inspect source provenance.
6. Review Aera's drafted award rationale.
7. Edit or accept the rationale.
8. Explicitly approve the award.
9. See the completed award record and its evidence trail.

Criteria change and no-award actions remain visible but disabled for this guided demo. They must explain the limitation directly when clicked.

## 4. Layout derived from sequence

- Header: one clear outcome-led title and supporting line.
- Decision basis: the approved RFx criteria and policy source, with a visible source link.
- Recommendation proposal: one supplier, recommendation summary, and live AI status.
- Trade-offs and evidence: two related sections; evidence follows each material claim and is inspectable.
- Draft rationale: editable buyer-facing rationale generated by Aera.
- Approval bar: explicit `Approve award` action; disabled until a proposal, valid supplier, and non-empty rationale exist.
- Completion state: award record ID and a compact list of preserved evidence references.

## 5. State contract

| State | Meaning | Allowed action |
|---|---|---|
| `comparison-not-ready` | Screen 4 has not produced an approved comparison basis | Return to comparison; no recommendation |
| `awaiting-recommendation` | Entry is valid; no proposal requested | Prepare award recommendation |
| `recommendation-in-progress` | Live AI is evaluating the approved comparison | Wait; approval unavailable |
| `recommendation-ready` | Proposal, criteria, trade-offs, evidence, and rationale exist | Review, edit rationale, approve |
| `recommendation-basis-missing` | AI cannot support a recommendation from the approved basis | Show exact missing basis and source; approval unavailable |
| `award-approval-in-progress` | Buyer approval is being committed | Wait; prevent duplicate commit |
| `award-approved` | Award record is committed | Show record; prevent duplicate approval |

## 6. AI behavior

- Use only the approved RFx, applicable policy, completed comparison, and buyer guidance.
- Apply the approved criteria and explain the resulting trade-offs.
- Return a proposal, not an approval.
- Draft a rationale that is grounded in the proposal and cited evidence.
- If the evaluation basis is missing or conflicting, identify the exact missing basis and do not recommend a supplier.
- Do not invent scores, weights, supplier values, provenance, or authority.

## 7. Deterministic behavior

- Verify Screen 4 readiness and acknowledgment before enabling recommendation.
- Validate supplier identity against the comparison.
- Validate criteria, comparison status, RFx/version, and non-empty rationale.
- Keep calculation and criteria state separate from model prose.
- Expose source links and evidence references returned by the proposal.
- Commit buyer approval through the award endpoint; generate a deterministic record ID and preserve the award payload.
- Prevent duplicate approval and show contextual errors inline.

## 8. Required demo assets

- Approved RFx PDF and version metadata.
- Approved procurement policy and award criteria clause.
- Completed comparison with normalized totals, trade-offs, exceptions, and line-level provenance.
- Five supplier response source sets.
- AI-generated recommendation proposal and draft rationale created at runtime from those assets.
- Award record returned by the approval endpoint.

## 9. Verification

- Screen 4 acknowledgment is required before entering this screen.
- Recommendation invokes the live AI endpoint and is not hardcoded.
- Recommendation contains a supplier, trade-offs, evidence, and rationale or exposes missing basis.
- Source links open the approved RFx, policy, and supplier evidence.
- Criteria change and no-award are visibly disabled with direct demo limitation copy.
- Empty rationale prevents approval.
- Approval invokes the award endpoint and shows a record ID.
- Refresh preserves the completed award state.
