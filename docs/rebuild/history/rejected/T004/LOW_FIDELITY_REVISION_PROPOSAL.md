# Proposed revision: Aera low-fidelity product model

Audience: product designer, product/software engineer, AI engineer, and reviewer.
Purpose: communicate the buyer’s expected user and information flow before visual design and technical design.
Authority: proposed correction to LOW_FIDELITY_PRODUCT_MODEL.md; it becomes active only after product-manager approval.

## Shared product flow

The buyer works in one sourcing thread. At every point, the product shows the current status, the next decision or action, the information needed to make it, and the evidence supporting material claims.

Aera proposals and buyer decisions are visibly distinct. A failed or incomplete operation does not erase the last valid state. The product communicates whether a result is proposed, waiting for buyer input, blocked, approved, simulated, or unavailable.

## Landing

The buyer understands the procurement job Aera supports, the main burden it reduces, and the path from business need to award. The buyer can start the guided sourcing thread.

## Create and approve RFx

The buyer provides or confirms the procurement ask. Aera uses the selected sources to produce a draft RFx containing scope, line items, questionnaire, terms, and response instructions.

The buyer can inspect the source basis, understand issues or assumptions, confirm their treatment, and approve the current RFx. The product makes clear that draft generation is not approval and that unresolved material issues prevent release.

## Lock supplier responses

The buyer can inspect the supplier contacts and the approved RFx package, initiate the outbound invitation through the available channel, and see the outcome separately for each supplier.

The product brings together each supplier’s messages, attachments, extracted response facts, unresolved points, and response history. Aera proposes a targeted follow-up; the buyer approves it; the next response version is reassessed without erasing earlier evidence. The buyer can confirm a supported treatment or see why the response remains unresolved.

The product makes clear that sending an invitation is not delivery, sending a follow-up is not resolution, and a response is not complete merely because a message exists.

## Compare responses

The buyer can inspect supplier offers on a common basis while retaining each original value, unit, currency, line-level claim, attachment, and source link. The product distinguishes missing, ambiguous, conflicting, and non-comparable information.

The buyer can ask questions about coverage, totals, delivery, quality, terms, and evidence. Aera returns a bounded answer with supporting evidence and limitations. The comparison view does not make or approve an award.

The buyer explicitly acknowledges that the comparison and analysis are sufficient for award recommendation before the next part of the thread becomes available.

## Decide and defend award

The buyer can inspect the approved evaluation basis, request Aera’s proposal, review the proposed supplier and trade-offs, inspect evidence, edit the rationale, and approve the award.

The product shows the approval as a buyer decision and returns a record result. Aera’s proposal never becomes an approval by itself.

## Designer handoff

The product designer should decide the visual language, hierarchy, spatial composition, responsive behavior, interaction polish, typography, color, motion, and use of existing or newly created visual assets. The design should preserve the flow, information requirements, evidence access, authority boundaries, and states above.

The low-fidelity model intentionally does not select a layout, breakpoint, visual asset, design system, or animation pattern.
