Northstar Manufacturing — Procurement Policy FY2026

Owner: Procurement Operations · Version 4.0 · Effective 1 April 2026 · Approved 20 March 2026

RFx requirements

Every RFx states specification, quantity, unit, location, required date, response deadline, and supplier-return requirements.

Commercial transparency

Unit price, currency, pricing basis, freight, taxes, payment terms, and validity are separate. Pricing basis is never inferred.

Quality

Suppliers identify grade, ply, dimensions, and test standard and provide current quality evidence. Missing evidence requires an approved Quality exception before qualification.

Normalization and currency

Unambiguous physical conversions may use the standard table. Multi-currency comparison uses the Finance-approved source and event rate date. Without treatment, preserve currency and keep comparison incomplete.

Award and authority

Unless the approved RFx says otherwise: comparable cost 50%, quality 30%, delivery 20%. Pricing-basis deviations require supplier correction or Procurement Operations approval. A Procurement Operations-approved exception may remain visible in the comparison with an explicit warning and provenance; it must be disclosed in any recommendation. Later delivery requires procurement-manager acceptance before award. AI recommendation is not award approval.
