Supplier questionnaire

  1. Can you supply all requested lines and quantities?
  2. Confirm grade, ply, dimensions, and test standard for each line.
  3. Can you meet delivery by 15 October 2026?
  4. Identify manufacturing site and legal supplier entity.
  5. Provide current quality certificate and test-report reference.
  6. Identify every deviation, substitution, exclusion, and assumption.
  7. Confirm production capacity for quarterly demand.
  8. State payment, validity, freight, taxes, currency, and pricing basis separately.
