Pune Plant Packaging Specification

Owner: Manufacturing Engineering · Revision 2026.2 · Issued 12 August 2026

Corrugated cartons are for finished-goods shipments from Pune. Dimensions are internal millimetres; quantities are quarterly; delivery is to the Pune receiving dock.

Suppliers state board grade, ply, dimensions, test standard, current quality certificate, test-report reference, currency, freight, taxes, payment, validity, and delivery. Every line is returned; unavailable lines are marked with a reason.
